Dubai 10-Day Itinerary Research Notes

Key Findings:
- Dubai offers blend of futuristic skyline, rich heritage, and desert adventures
- Typical 10-day structure: Arrival, Downtown Dubai, Old Dubai, Beach/Marina, Desert Safari, Museums, Abu Dhabi day trip, Adventure parks, Relaxation/shopping, Departure
- Famous landmarks: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Dubai Fountain, Palm Jumeirah
- Cultural experiences: Al Fahidi Historic District, Dubai Museum, Museum of the Future, Etihad Museum, Jumeirah Mosque
- Day trip: Abu Dhabi (Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, Qasr Al Watan)
- Transport: Metro, taxi, bus, rideshare, abras
- Budget range: AED 650-1,250/day depending on comfort level
