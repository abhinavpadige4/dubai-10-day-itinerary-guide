# 10-Day Dubai Itinerary: Comprehensive Travel Guide

## Overview
This comprehensive 10-day Dubai itinerary covers iconic landmarks, cultural experiences, modern attractions, desert adventures, and a day trip to Abu Dhabi. Includes detailed daily schedules, food recommendations, transport tips, and budget breakdowns.

**Total Estimated Budget (per person, mid-range): AED 8,500 - 12,000 (~USD 2,300 - 3,250)**
- Accommodation: AED 300-500/night (3-star hotel) or AED 800+/night (4-5 star)
- Food: AED 150-250/day
- Transport: AED 50-100/day
- Attractions/Activities: AED 100-300/day
- Miscellaneous: AED 50-100/day

## Day-by-Day Schedule

### **Day 1: Arrival & Downtown Dubai Introduction**
**Theme:** Settling in and first impressions of modern Dubai

**Morning:**
- Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB)
- Transfer to hotel (recommended areas: Deira, Bur Dubai, or Downtown Dubai)
- Check-in and freshen up

**Afternoon:**
- Light lunch at hotel or nearby café (try Al Mallah for authentic shawarma - AED 12)
- Visit Dubai Mall (world's largest shopping mall)
- Explore the aquarium and underwater zoo (AED 120)

**Evening:**
- Watch the free Dubai Fountain show (shows every 30 minutes from 6 PM-11 PM)

- Optional: Burj Khalifa At the Top sunset tickets (pre-booked - AED 149)

**Transport:** Taxi from airport to hotel (AED 50-70) or Metro (Red Line) + taxi (AED 15-25)
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 25-35
**Food Budget:** AED 80-120
**Attractions Budget:** AED 120-270
**Total Day 1 Estimate:** AED 225-425

## Practical Tips Section

### **Visa Requirements**
- **GCC Citizens:** Visa-free entry
- **Many Nationalities:** Visa on arrival (30-90 days, check eligibility)
- **Others:** Pre-arranged visa required (apply through UAE embassy, hotel, or airline)
- **Passport Validity:** Minimum 6 months from entry date
- **Cost:** Visa on arrival typically free or AED 100-300 depending on nationality and duration

### **Weather & Best Time to Visit**
- **Peak Season:** November to March (pleasant weather: 20-30°C / 68-86°F)
- **Shoulder Season:** April & October (warming up: 25-35°C / 77-95°F)
- **Summer:** May to September (very hot: 35-45°C / 95-113°F, high humidity)
- **Ramadan:** Dates vary yearly - be respectful of fasting hours (no eating/drinking in public during daylight)

### **Dress Code & Cultural Etiquette**
- **Modest Clothing:** Recommended in public areas (shoulders and knees covered)
- **Beachwear:** Acceptable at beaches, pools, and water parks
- **Mosques:** Modest dress required (women: headscarf, long clothing; men: long pants)
- **Ramadan:** Avoid eating, drinking, smoking in public during daylight hours
- **Public Displays of Affection:** Keep minimal (holding hands generally ok, kissing/hugging not advised)
- **Photography:** Ask permission before photographing people, especially women; avoid government buildings

### **Currency & Payments**
- **Currency:** UAE Dirham (AED) - pegged to USD (1 USD = 3.67 AED)
- **Cards:** Credit/debit cards widely accepted (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- **Cash:** Keep small amounts for markets, taxis, and small establishments
- **ATMs:** Widely available; notify your bank of travel plans
- **Tipping:** 10-15% in restaurants if service charge not included; AED 5-10 for hotel staff, taxi drivers

### **Language & Communication**
- **Official Language:** Arabic
- **Widely Spoken:** English (especially in tourism, business, expat areas)
- **Useful Arabic Phrases:**
  - Hello: Marhaban / Salam alaikum
  - Thank you: Shukran
  - Please: Min fadlak (to male) / Min fadlik (to female)
  - Excuse me/Sorry: Afwan
  - How much?: Kam ath-thaman?
- **SIM Cards:** Purchase at airport (du, Etisalat, Virgin Mobile) - AED 50-100 for tourist packages with data

### **Transportation Guide**
- **Metro:** Red and Green lines - efficient, air-conditioned, AED 3-8.50 per journey
  - NOL Card: Reusable smart card (AED 25 for card + minimum balance)
  - Gold Class: Premium metro cars (2x standard fare)
- **Bus:** Extensive network - AED 3-8.50 per journey (same NOL Card)
- **Tram:** Dubai Marina and Al Sufouh - AED 3 per journey
- **Taxi:** Metered (start AED 5, then AED 1.96/km) - reliable and relatively inexpensive
- **Rideshare:** Uber and Careem widely available (often slightly cheaper than taxis)
- **Water Transport:** Abras (AED 1), water taxis, ferries
- **Car Rental:** Available but traffic and parking can be challenging; international license required

### **Health & Safety**
- **Safety:** Dubai is very safe for tourists; low crime rate
- **Health:** Excellent medical facilities; travel insurance recommended
- **Water:** Tap water is safe but most prefer bottled water (AED 2-5)
- **Pharmacies:** Widely available (look for green cross sign)
- **Emergency:** 999 for police, ambulance, or fire
- **Sun Protection:** High SPF sunscreen, hat, sunglasses essential year-round

### **Shopping Tips**
- **Tax-Free Shopping:** Available for tourists at participating stores (minimum spend AED 250)
- **Bargaining:** Expected in souks (Gold, Spice, Textile); fixed prices in malls
- **Shopping Festles:** Dubai Shopping Festival (Dec-Jan) and Dubai Summer Surprises (Jun-Aug)
- **Best Souvenirs:** Dates, Arabic coffee sets, gold jewelry, perfumes, textiles, spices, handicrafts

### **Cultural Experiences Not to Miss**
- **Traditional Meal:** Try Emirati cuisine (machboos, harees, luqaimat)
- **Coffee Ceremony:** Experience Arabic coffee serving with dates
- **Henna Painting:** Available at souks, malls, and cultural centers
- **Falconry Displays:** Often part of desert safari or cultural shows
- **Traditional Music:** Listen for oud music in traditional venues

### **Day Trip Specific Tips (Abu Dhabi)**
- **Depart Early:** Maximize your time in Abu Dhabi
- **Dress Modestly:** Especially for mosque visit (cover shoulders, knees, women cover head)
- **Friday Timings:** Some attractions have different hours on Fridays (Islamic holy day)
- **Heat:** Even in cooler months, sun can be intense - carry water and sunscreen
- **Return Traffic:** Allow extra time for return to Dubai (especially Friday evenings)

### **Budget-Saving Tips**
- **Accommodation:** Consider areas like Deira, Bur Dubai, or slightly outside Downtown for better rates
- **Food:** Mix of fine dining and local eateries (try Lebanese, Indian, Pakistani restaurants for value)
- **Attractions:** Look for combo tickets, online discounts, and free attractions (fountains, beaches, souks)
- **Transport:** Use NOL card for public transport; consider day passes if using extensively
- **Water:** Carry refillable water bottle; refill at hotels or malls
- **Timing:** Visit popular attractions early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds and heat

### **Emergency Information**
- **Police:** 999
- **Ambulance:** 999
- **Fire:** 999
- **Tourist Police:** Available in major tourist areas (speak multiple languages)
- **Your Country's Embassy:** Keep contact information handy
- **Hotel Address:** Keep written copy in case of phone issues

### **Packing Suggestions**
- **Clothing:** Light, breathable fabrics; modest options for cultural sites; swimwear
- **Footwear:** Comfortable walking shoes; sandals/flip-flops for beach; dressier options for fine dining
- **Essentials:** Sunscreen (SPF 50+), hat, sunglasses, reusable water bottle, adapter (UK-type 3-pin)
- **Electronics:** Phone, charger, camera, power bank
- **Documents:** Passport, visa, travel insurance, hotel confirmations, emergency contacts
- **Extras:** Small day bag, tissues/wet wipes, hand sanitizer, any personal medications

## Important Notes & Disclaimers

1. **Prices:** All prices are approximate and in UAE Dirhams (AED). Actual costs may vary based on season, availability, and personal choices.
2. **Attraction Hours:** Always check current opening hours and book tickets in advance for popular attractions (especially Burj Khalifa, Museum of the Future, desert safaris).
3. **Seasonal Variations:** Some attractions (like Global Village) are seasonal; desert safaris may be less comfortable in peak summer.
4. **Cultural Sensitivity:** Respect local customs and laws; when in doubt, err on the side of modesty and discretion.
5. **Health:** Stay hydrated; Dubai's climate can be deceptively dehydrating even when you don't feel hot.
6. **Flexibility:** This itinerary is a guide - feel free to swap days based on flight schedules, weather, or personal preferences.
7. **Booking:** For best prices and availability, book accommodation and major attractions 2-4 weeks in advance, especially during peak season.
